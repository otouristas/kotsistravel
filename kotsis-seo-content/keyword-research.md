# Keyword research — kotsistravel.gr

Source: Ahrefs. Volumes are monthly; KD is Ahrefs Keyword Difficulty (0–100).
A dash means the metric was not returned for that term.

## Starting position

kotsistravel.gr has effectively **no non-brand organic visibility**. The homepage
carries roughly **144 referring domains** — a real asset for a site this size.

That combination defines the strategy: the domain has authority but no pages
pointed at demand. This is a migration-and-targeting problem, not a link problem.

## The core finding

Every Greece↔Italy ferry route query has **KD 0** in every language, while the
head terms above them are 65–71. Nobody owns the route-level queries.

## Build order

1. **Greek ferry cluster** — home market, 1,160 combined monthly volume at KD 0
2. **German ferry** — 1,450/mo at KD 0, highest single opportunity
3. **Turkish ferry** — 920/mo at KD 0, likely uncontested
4. **Italian ferry** — 380/mo, highest intent
5. **English ferry** — `x-default`, plus AI answer engines
6. Greek commercial (insurance, cruises, packages, tours)
7. FR / BG / ES / SQ / RU

## Terms to avoid

- **Coach, bus, transfer and fleet queries** — those belong to `kotsis.travel`.
  Targeting them here splits the two domains against each other.
- **Dated queries** (`πακέτα διακοπών 2025`) — a permanent URL with a year in it
  decays. Use seasonal pages only where inventory genuinely exists, and unpublish
  them afterwards.
- **Fabricated long-tail** — do not build a page per airline or per hotel unless
  there is demand behind it. The legacy site's 35 airline pages and 40 hotel
  listicles are exactly the mistake being corrected.
